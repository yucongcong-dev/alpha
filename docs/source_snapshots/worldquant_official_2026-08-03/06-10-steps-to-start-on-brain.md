## Content

10 Steps to Start on BRAIN
Watch the “Introduction to Alphas” training series
Read the starter pack series under “Discover BRAIN” section
Simulate three simple formulas using the data field: close and the operator: rank
Understand the simulation settings, starting with delay and neutralization .
Click the Example button on the bottom left of the Simulate page for examples. Try to improve these expressions using the Hint and then submit
Understand the Simulation Results and submission tests.
Simulate Alphas discussed in the "Create Alphas" sections Run your First Alpha and Another Sample Alpha. Pay attention to the settings.
Check the Events page to attend training webinars.
Read the BRAIN TIPS series and the Beginner posts in FAQs/ Forums
Try to create Alphas using available Price volume data fields or and, Operators
